Vocabite Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026.

This policy explains how Vocabite processes data when you use the Android app (com.vocabite.myapp) and vocabite.jeyasclub.com. Vocabite helps you look up words, save a Pocket collection, practice, and share a learning profile.

1. Operator and privacy contact

Vocabite is operated by Jeya’s Club. For privacy questions, corrections, access requests, or account deletion, contact jeyasclub@gmail.com. Never send your Google password or sign-in tokens.

2. Data we process

  • Google account data: account identifiers, name, email address, email verification status, and profile photo when provided by Google. The authentication system may store OAuth tokens and their expiry times to support sign-in. Vocabite does not receive your Google password.
  • Pocket data: saved words or phrases, meanings, translations, examples, sources, personal notes, dates added, practice results, and review schedules. Previously saved learning preferences may also be retained.
  • Profile data: display name, username, bio, avatar accessories, Pocket design, and public or private status. Word counts and achievements are calculated from learning data. Friend requests and accepted friendships are stored to support the Friends feature.
  • Lookup data: the word or phrase you submit to retrieve dictionary results and translations.
  • Subscription data: purchase identifiers and tokens, subscription status, and expiry information used to verify Premium access.
  • Technical data: session information, access times, IP addresses, browser or device information sent with network requests, and error records needed to operate and protect the service. Hosting providers may also process network data to secure and operate their infrastructure.
  • On-device data: app sessions, cached Pocket data, preferences, and unsynced drafts. Export files are created only when you choose to export data.

The Android app does not currently request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location. Pronunciation uses Android text-to-speech and does not record your voice.

3. How we use data

We use data to authenticate accounts, keep each user’s Pocket separate, sync collections across devices, provide word lookups, schedule practice, calculate learning progress and achievements, support friendships, verify Premium access, and display profiles you choose to share. Technical data supports maintenance, troubleshooting, and prevention of unauthorized access.

The current implementation does not include advertising or marketing analytics SDKs. The app does not use account or Pocket data to sell audiences or target advertisements.

4. Dictionaries and third-party services

Vocabite uses the following providers depending on the feature and service availability:

  • Google: account authentication through Google Sign-In, Indonesian translations through Google Cloud Translation, and Android subscription purchases through Google Play. These interactions are also subject to Google’s policies.
  • Cloudflare: API processing, account and Pocket storage in D1, and network security.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting. Its infrastructure receives network requests from your browser when you visit the website.
  • Merriam-Webster, Datamuse, Wiktionary (including Wikikamus), and Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev): lookup words or phrases are sent to retrieve definitions, synonyms, translations, or references. Providers may vary between lookups. Definitions and examples come from dictionary sources.
  • The active Android text-to-speech service: receives text you choose to hear. Processing may happen on your device or over the network, depending on the speech engine and its settings.

Lookup requests do not include your email, sign-in tokens, or entire Pocket. Avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information as lookup text.

Providers may process data outside your country of residence. Their practices, processing locations, and retention follow their own services and terms. Vocabite does not promise that all data is stored in Indonesia.

5. Public profiles and sharing

Your profile becomes available to others when you enable Public profile and set a username. Public profiles may display your name, username, bio, avatar, Pocket design, word and friend counts, achievements, and cards containing words, meanings, and translations. People with the link can view it without signing in. Other users can find public profiles by username.

Your account email, session tokens, personal notes, private card sources, and personal examples are not included in public profile responses. Making a profile public does not share all account data.

You can edit your profile or turn off public sharing in the profile editor. Disabling sharing restricts subsequent access through Vocabite but cannot recall copies or screenshots made by others.

If you export your Pocket or share a link through Android, your chosen app or destination receives that file or link. Exports may contain personal notes; store and share them carefully.

6. Storage and security

Account, Pocket, and profile data are stored on the backend for use across devices. App-to-backend communication uses HTTPS, and Pocket access is checked against the signed-in account. On Android, session and Pocket/draft copies are encrypted using Android Keystore. These preferences are excluded from device backup and transfer through the app configuration.

On the website, session cookies and browser storage support sign-in and sync drafts. Browser storage does not use Android Keystore. Native session tokens are stored as hashes in the database. These safeguards reduce risk but cannot make devices or services immune to every security incident. You are also responsible for protecting access to your device and Google account.

7. Retention

Account, Pocket, and profile data are retained to provide the service until deleted. The current version does not automatically delete accounts solely for inactivity. Delete cards you no longer want and wait for successful sync so the server records the change.

Native sign-in tickets are valid for 10 minutes and native sessions for 30 days. Expiry limits authentication use; it does not guarantee immediate deletion of the database row. Expired tickets and sessions are cleaned up when a new sign-in ticket is created. Signing out attempts to revoke the current native session.

Android caches and drafts may remain after sign-out to preserve unsynced changes. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-managed local copies but does not delete your account or server Pocket. Delete exported files separately. Browser data can be cleared through browser settings.

Backups, infrastructure logs, and data received by providers may follow provider retention periods. This policy does not promise immediate deletion of every such copy. If data must be retained for legal or security reasons, we will explain its scope and the reason when responding to the relevant request.

8. Your choices and account deletion

You can edit your profile and sharing settings, delete Pocket cards, and sign out. Deleting cards, signing out, or revoking Vocabite’s Google access does not itself delete your Vocabite account.

To delete your account, open the Delete account page and follow the confirmation steps. You can also request access, correction, or deletion by emailing the privacy contact from the address used to sign in. For deletion requests, use the subject “Vocabite account deletion request”. Ownership verification may be required; passwords and sign-in tokens are not needed.

Account deletion includes your Vocabite identity, sessions and sign-in links, Pocket, practice history, profile, and friendships. It does not delete your Google account or recall copies you exported or shared with others. Save or export anything you want to keep before deleting your account. Manage any Google Play subscription separately in Google Play.

9. Children’s privacy

Vocabite does not ask for a date of birth or verify age through the current interface. Parents or guardians concerned about a child’s data can contact us for review. Do not share children’s personal information through public profiles or lookup text.

10. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when features, providers, or data processing change. The latest version will state its update date. Changes requiring additional notice or consent will be communicated through the service before taking effect, as required.
